🔥 Cinematic Matchstick Throw — Explosion Scene

For a 10-second cinematic video. The action must begin with empty hands: walk, take the matchbox from a pocket, remove and light one match, throw it backward, then continue walking as the explosion happens behind you.

Copy-ready prompt

Maintain the exact face identity, facial features, hairstyle, moustache, sunglasses, black full-sleeve shirt, olive-green cargo pants, scarf, wristwatch and black tactical boots from Reference Image 1. SCENE: A deserted asphalt road at night, symmetrical rows of streetlights fading into light mist. One continuous full-body tracking shot. The subject walks calmly toward the camera. STRICT ACTION SEQUENCE — DO NOT SKIP OR REORDER: 0–2 seconds — WALKING: The subject walks forward naturally with both hands empty and relaxed. No matchstick, no flame, and no matchbox visible in either hand. 2–4 seconds — TAKE OUT MATCHBOX: While continuing to walk, he reaches into his trouser pocket, takes out a small matchbox, opens it, and removes one wooden matchstick. Show this entire action clearly. 4–6 seconds — LIGHT THE MATCH: He strikes the wooden matchstick against the striking strip on the matchbox. The match ignites at its tip with a small realistic flame. Clearly show the wooden stick between his fingers and the flame attached to its tip. 6–7 seconds — THROW THE MATCH: While walking forward, he flicks his right hand backward and releases the actual burning matchstick behind him. Clearly show the stick leaving his fingertips, traveling backward through the air, and his hand becoming empty. 7–8 seconds — EXPLOSION: Only after the matchstick has visibly been thrown, a large cinematic explosion erupts in the distant background. Bright orange fire, rolling smoke, glowing embers and dramatic backlighting fill the road. 8–10 seconds — HERO WALK: The subject continues walking toward the camera with a calm, fearless expression. He never turns around or looks at the explosion. Preserve his exact face and clothing. VISUAL STYLE: Photorealistic Hollywood action-film cinematography, 35mm anamorphic lens, realistic object movement, natural hand motion, volumetric smoke, warm rim lighting, high contrast, detailed textures, 10-second video. STRICT NEGATIVE PROMPT: Do not start with a matchstick in either hand. No pre-lit match. Do not skip taking the matchbox from the pocket. No flame before the match is struck. No explosion before the matchstick is thrown. No teleporting or disappearing objects, no extra fingers, no face changes, no scene cuts. Prioritize the correct action order and visible object continuity over the explosion's size.
