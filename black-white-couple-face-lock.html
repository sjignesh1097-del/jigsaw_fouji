Black & White Couple Portrait — Strict Face Lock

Use one clear reference photo of each person. This prompt prioritizes recognizable facial identity, a cinematic monochrome look, and a clean image without any clock, date, or text.

Your Prompt

PROMPT: STRICT FACE IDENTITY LOCK — BLACK & WHITE ROMANTIC COUPLE PORTRAIT REFERENCE IDENTITY — HIGHEST PRIORITY: Use the uploaded reference photo of the man as the only source for the man's identity and the uploaded reference photo of the woman as the only source for the woman's identity. Preserve each person's recognizable real facial features as faithfully as the image-generation model allows. FACE PRESERVATION: - Keep each person's original face shape, forehead, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, cheeks, jawline, chin, ears, natural skin tone, and distinctive features. - Preserve the man's original moustache, its shape and curled ends, facial hair, hairline, and natural expression as shown in his reference photo. - Preserve the woman's original face, eyes, smile, hairline, bindi, and other visible distinctive features as shown in her reference photo. - Do not mix, merge, swap, or transfer facial features between the two people. - Do not invent substitute faces or apply beauty filters, face slimming, skin smoothing, age changes, or artificial facial enhancements. - Keep both people recognizable as the same individuals from their reference photos. If a stylistic effect conflicts with identity preservation, prioritize the original facial features. SCENE AND COMPOSITION: Create a photorealistic, intimate black-and-white couple portrait inspired by a premium cinematic editorial photograph. The man is in the foreground on the right, wearing a simple black collared shirt, his head slightly tilted down with a calm, natural expression. The woman stands close behind his left shoulder, her face visible beside him, looking toward the camera with a gentle, affectionate expression. Keep their heads close together and the pose natural. Use the reference photos only for identity; the scene description guides the pose and styling. LIGHTING AND PHOTOGRAPHY: Monochrome black-and-white tones, dark softly blurred background, low-key cinematic lighting, gentle silver highlights, deep blacks, natural skin texture, realistic hair strands, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, professional portrait photography, detailed but not over-sharpened. ALLOWED CHANGES: Pose, body position, clothing, lighting, background, camera angle, framing, and black-and-white color grading only. Do not intentionally redesign either person's face. OUTPUT: Vertical 9:16 portrait, close-up composition, realistic anatomy, high-resolution photographic appearance. NEGATIVE PROMPT: different identity, altered face, face swap, face morphing, mixed facial features, changed eyes, changed nose, changed lips, altered jawline, artificial beauty filter, plastic skin, excessive retouching, cartoon, illustration, distorted anatomy, extra people, text, letters, numbers, clock, time, date, watermark, phone interface, borders. IMPORTANT: No clock, no time, no date, and no text anywhere in the image. Facial identity preservation is the top priority, but exact identity matching depends on the image-generation tool.
