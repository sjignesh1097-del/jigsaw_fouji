🌼 Trending Saree Multi-Pose Collage

AI Image Prompt · Instagram Reels/Stories friendly · 9:16 vertical

Copy-ready prompt

Create a high-resolution, photorealistic 9:16 vertical Instagram portrait collage using the uploaded reference photo as the ONLY identity reference for the woman. STRICT FACE IDENTITY LOCK: Preserve her recognizable facial identity from the reference photo as closely as possible—same face shape, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone, and natural facial proportions. Do not substitute a different person or create an idealized/altered face. Keep the result natural and realistic. DESIGN: Show three coordinated portrait cutouts of the same woman in a stylish staggered collage. The largest portrait is in the upper center; two smaller portraits sit across the lower half with different natural poses, such as gently touching her hair and resting her cheek on her hand. All three portraits must clearly depict the same person, with consistent face, hair, and styling. OUTFIT & BEAUTY: A pale butter-yellow saree with delicate multicolored floral embroidery and scalloped embroidered edging; matching blouse; long, dark, softly styled hair with a few subtle pearl-like hair accents; a small white daisy tucked behind one ear; natural makeup, red lipstick, a small bindi, and tasteful traditional jewelry and green bangles. Keep the styling elegant and believable. BACKGROUND & GRAPHIC STYLE: Warm cream paper texture with a golden-yellow and soft orange arch behind the main portrait. Add white daisies, delicate botanical leaves, tiny hand-drawn hearts, and minimal decorative doodles. Give each portrait a clean white sticker-style outline with tasteful layered scrapbook composition. Use soft flattering light, sharp facial details, realistic skin texture, balanced colors, and polished professional graphic design. COMPOSITION: Full 9:16 vertical frame, high detail, crisp edges, harmonious spacing, portraits not awkwardly cropped, no text, no watermark, no logo, no extra people, no duplicated facial features, and no inconsistent identities between the three portraits. Use the uploaded photo to preserve identity; the saree, poses, and background may be changed to match this design.
